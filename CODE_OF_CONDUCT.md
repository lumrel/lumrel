# Lumrel Code of Conduct

Lumrel aims to maintain a technical community where people can collaborate productively, disagree constructively, and contribute without harassment or intimidation.

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, discussions, pull requests, issues, documentation, community spaces, and other activities conducted on behalf of the Lumrel project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- discuss ideas rather than attack people
- give technical feedback constructively
- assume good faith when reasonable
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them
- respect different levels of experience
- keep discussions relevant and productive
- respect privacy and confidentiality
- give credit for other people's work
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions even when disagreement remains

Technical disagreement is welcome.

Personal hostility is not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment
- threats
- intimidation
- personal attacks
- discriminatory or demeaning remarks
- sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention
- deliberate humiliation
- repeated disruptive behavior
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- impersonation
- malicious manipulation of project processes
- sustained off-topic disruption
- retaliation against someone who reports misconduct

Criticism of code, architecture, APIs, documentation, or technical decisions is not harassment when expressed professionally.

## Technical Discussions

Strong technical opinions are normal in software engineering.

Participants should be able to say:

> I think this API is unsafe because...

or:

> I disagree with this architecture because...

without turning the discussion into:

> You clearly do not understand Rust.

Focus criticism on the implementation, design, assumptions, evidence, or trade-offs.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct within project spaces.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

- request a change in behavior
- edit or remove inappropriate content
- lock discussions
- close issues or pull requests
- restrict participation
- temporarily suspend participation
- permanently ban a participant

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Serious threats, harassment, or deliberate abuse may justify immediate action.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the project maintainers using an available private communication channel.

Do not publish sensitive personal information when reporting misconduct.

Reports should include enough context for maintainers to understand what happened.

## Confidentiality

Maintainers should handle conduct reports with reasonable confidentiality.

Information may be shared when necessary to investigate the report, protect participants, comply with applicable law, or enforce project policies.

Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Conflicts Involving Maintainers

When a report concerns a maintainer, that maintainer should not make the enforcement decision about their own case when another appropriate maintainer is available.

As the maintainer team grows, Lumrel may establish a more formal escalation process.

## Scope Outside Project Spaces

Behavior outside official Lumrel spaces may be considered when it directly affects the safety of project participants or represents the project in an official capacity.

The project does not attempt to police unrelated private behavior.

## Good-Faith Reports

Good-faith reports will not be treated as misconduct merely because an investigation does not confirm a violation.

Intentionally false or malicious reports may themselves violate this Code of Conduct.

## Changes

This Code of Conduct may evolve as the Lumrel community grows.

Changes should preserve the project's goal of maintaining a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming environment.
