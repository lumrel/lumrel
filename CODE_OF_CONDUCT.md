# Lumrel Code of Conduct

Lumrel aims to maintain a technical community where people can collaborate productively, disagree constructively, and contribute without harassment or intimidation.

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, community spaces, and activities conducted on behalf of Lumrel.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- discuss ideas rather than attack people
- give technical feedback constructively
- assume good faith when reasonable
- acknowledge mistakes
- respect different experience levels
- keep discussions relevant
- respect privacy
- give credit for other people's work
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions even when disagreement remains

Technical disagreement is welcome.

Personal hostility is not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment
- threats
- intimidation
- personal attacks
- discriminatory or demeaning remarks
- sexual harassment
- deliberate humiliation
- repeated disruptive behavior
- publishing private information without permission
- impersonation
- malicious manipulation of project processes
- sustained off-topic disruption
- retaliation against people reporting misconduct

Criticism of code, architecture, APIs, documentation, or technical decisions is not harassment when expressed professionally.

## Technical Discussion

Strong technical opinions are normal.

Participants may say:

> I think this API is unsafe because...

or:

> I disagree with this architecture because...

without turning the discussion into personal criticism.

Focus on:

- implementation
- evidence
- assumptions
- trade-offs
- maintainability
- security
- compatibility

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct within project spaces.

Depending on severity, maintainers may:

- request a change in behavior
- remove inappropriate content
- lock discussions
- close issues or pull requests
- restrict participation
- temporarily suspend participation
- permanently ban a participant

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers.

Do not publish sensitive personal details in public issues.

Provide enough context for maintainers to understand what happened.

## Confidentiality

Maintainers should handle reports with reasonable confidentiality.

Information may be shared where necessary to investigate an incident, protect participants, enforce policy, or comply with applicable law.

Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Conflicts Involving Maintainers

When a report concerns a maintainer, that person should not decide their own case when another appropriate maintainer is available.

A more formal escalation process may be introduced as the maintainer team grows.

## Good-Faith Reports

Good-faith reports are not misconduct merely because an investigation does not confirm a violation.

Intentionally false or malicious reports may themselves violate this Code of Conduct.

## Changes

This policy may evolve as the Lumrel community grows while preserving the goal of respectful, technically rigorous collaboration.
